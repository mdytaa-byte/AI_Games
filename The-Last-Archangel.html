The Last Archangel · Book II: The Last Kingdom · Book III: The Last Dawn
by Michael D. Young
A 16-bit chronicle of the Destroying Angel,
the cherub Big Halo, and the End of Time.

How to Play

Arrow keys / WASD move. Z, Enter, or Space confirms. X or Esc opens the menu and cancels. In the menu, up and down change pages. When a list appears, up and down choose, Z confirms, X leaves.

You are Xandir, a Watcher sentenced to serve as Destroying Angel until the End of Time. Walk the maps, speak with every soul, and fight when darkness bars the way.

Ambrosia restores the body. Serche water restores the spirit. Manna is the coin of both Heaven and Nod.

The tale follows the trilogy: Pompeii and the Fortuna house; Jarom and Clara against Beelzebub; Olympus, Cain, and the All-Seeing Eye.

Isda the Whisperer waits in the Cherub Fortress. Ask her, then choose a road: Nod, the Forgotten Tower, the Vault of Avarice, London, Blacker Forest, or Nineveh.

Only four may walk. From the Party page, Z changes who marches. Yearti will wait if the road is already full.

Combat is a conversation with knives. Judy’s Purify clears burn and venom. Sparkslinger sets the house alight. The Tree of Death offers fruit. If you brought Druiel’s extra links, they tighten around the Prince each round.
