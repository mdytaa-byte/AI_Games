How to Play

From the novel: Trifecta is a three-player contest on a triangular board of light and dark spaces, with keeps at its tips. Pieces differ in ability and physical size. Capture is by surrounding. The Mage Gigas is a colossal, hovering, three-section creature whose black armor shimmers red, blue, and yellow. Reaching a rival keep defeats its ruler.

The chamber

  1. Turns proceed Ember, Azure, then Dorian. On your turn, move one piece, rotate a multi-space piece, or change one Mage Fowl’s aspect.
  2. Light and dark triangular tiles alternate. Ordinary steps always change color. Sight leaps two links and therefore stays on the same color—until the Fowl changes aspect.
  3. Every cell of a multi-space piece must fit on empty tiles. Larger creatures may also rotate sixty degrees around one of their vertices.
  4. After acting, your adjacent pieces exert surrounding pressure. Mage Fowl require two pressure to capture, Mage Beasts three, Mage Bears four, and the Mage Gigas five. Strength counts as two pressure; the Gigas counts as three.
  5. Keeps are sacred. No army may rest on a keep. If any part of a piece reaches a rival keep—or stands adjacent and storms the gate—that faction wins immediately. Sight Fowl may also leap the last two links.
  6. Capture every rival piece to eliminate that faction. The last faction standing also wins.
◆ StrengthOne linked step. Counts as two surrounding pressure, and creatures with Strength resist capture more stubbornly in spirit—use it to finish a surround.
➶ SwiftnessMay travel through up to two linked, empty spaces in one move. Mage Beasts are born with this hunt.
◉ SightMay leap directly to an empty space exactly two links away, ignoring the tile between—the assassin’s path into a keep.

Armies: Each faction commands five one-space Mage Fowl, one two-space Mage Beast (Swiftness), and one three-space Mage Bear (Strength). The unique Mage Gigas begins unbound in the center as three hovering sections. Surround it with five pressure to take command; another faction may later seize it. Because it hovers, the Gigas may fly one or two lattice steps, rotate like other large creatures, and land on any empty matching footprint, passing over intervening creatures.
