How to Play

From the novel: Trifecta has three players, a triangular board of light and dark spaces, keeps at its tips, pieces with different abilities and physical sizes, and capture by surrounding. The Mage Gigas is a colossal, hovering, three-section creature whose black armor shimmers red, blue, and yellow. Reaching a rival keep defeats its ruler.

Playable adaptation

  1. Turns proceed Ember, Azure, then Dorian. Move one piece or change one Mage Fowl’s power.
  2. Ordinary movement follows one engraved link into an empty space. Every cell of a multi-space piece must fit at its destination.
  3. After acting, your adjacent pieces exert surrounding pressure. Mage Fowl require two pressure to capture, Mage Beasts require three, Mage Bears require four, and the Mage Gigas requires five. Strength counts as two pressure.
  4. Capture every rival piece to eliminate that faction. Enter either rival keep to win immediately.
◆ StrengthCounts as two surrounding pressure. Creatures with Strength also resist capture.
➶ SwiftnessMay travel through up to two linked, empty spaces in one move.
◉ SightMay leap directly to an empty space exactly two links away.

Expanded armies: Each faction commands five one-space Mage Fowl, one two-space Mage Beast, and one three-space Mage Bear. The unique seven-space Mage Gigas begins neutral. Surround it with five pressure to take command; another faction may later seize it. The Gigas hovers one linked step, contributes three surrounding pressure, and wins normally if any part reaches a rival keep.
