A Wonderful Week for a Quest · Book One

A prophecy. A knight. A goatherd.

Zero chance this goes according to plan.*

Meet an unlucky bard, an overconfident knight, and a questing party that may be less qualified than the monsters they face. Book One is a complete, family-friendly comedic fantasy—and the easiest door into Michael D. Young’s world.

* There will be footnotes.

Jackdaw, washed-up wizard and Merlin’s third cousin, refuses to stay politely outside the story. The footnotes are half the fun.

Why readers stay

Funny without losing the adventure

Clean comedy, found-family quests, and a narrator who knows the rules of storytelling—then declines to follow them quietly.

❧

Clean comedy

All-ages humor, playful fantasy satire, and jokes that reward reading aloud. No grimdark required.

⚔

Found-family quests

Lovable misfits discovering that heroism is rarely a solo activity—and almost never goes to plan.

✎

Footnotes with opinions

Wizard-narrator Jackdaw interferes, annotates, and generally refuses to remain outside the margins.

The questing party

Less qualified than the monsters they face

A mixed-up prophecy sends a gallant knight and a sub-assistant goatherd chasing the same heroic destiny. Their help includes a washed-up wizard, an un-distressed damsel, and a diminutive dark lord with very large plans.

Meet the author

Utah author. Choir member. Recovering community-theater ham.

Michael D. Young writes family-friendly fantasy, humorous adventures, picture books, devotionals, and music history. He lives in Utah with his family, creates online curriculum, and has sung with the Tabernacle Choir at Temple Square since 2010.
