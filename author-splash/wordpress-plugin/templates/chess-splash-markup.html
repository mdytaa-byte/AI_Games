Chess Quest · Book One · Ages 10–14

Seven days. Four trials.

The pawn has your initials.

Rich Witz is just trying to survive middle school—until his grandmother disappears and leaves behind a white chess pawn carved with his name. Overnight he is chosen as the next Paladin. Complete four trials of valor in seven days, or the game ends.
