Who is David Michaels?

Same jokes. Smaller page count.

Yes, you’re in the right place. David Michaels is the picture-book pen name of Michael D. Young—author of A Wonderful Week for a Quest, seventeen(ish) produced musicals, and a shelf of devotionals. Two decades in the Tabernacle Choir at Temple Square is excellent training for books meant to be performed out loud. He lives in Utah, where the mythical characters are mostly well-employed.
