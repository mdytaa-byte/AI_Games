A new independent imprint

The book, the hill, the morning light

A barrow is an old English hill — an ancient rise that holds memory. Brightbarrow is the moment a path climbs it: a book opening in the hands, a reader finding the way, a sun coming up over the next page.

We are a small literary imprint. We look for stories with craft in the spine and light on the hill: journeys, wonder, and hope that has done its homework. Our first titles are being set. This page is the door.

The book

Pages meant to be kept

Careful editing, considered design, and books that still feel like objects — even when they begin as files.

The path

Stories of becoming

We publish work that travels: characters who change, questions that linger, and plots that earn their last hill.

The light

Warmth with a backbone

Hope is welcome here. Sentimentality is not. We want books that leave a reader steadier than they arrived.

The first list

We will write when the catalog opens

No daily newsletter. No fuss. A note when the first Brightbarrow titles appear, and the occasional letter after that. Confirm by email; leave whenever you like.

Until the imprint site has its own list, this uses the reader newsletter at authormichaelyoung.com. You can also write mdytaa@gmail.com.

For writers

Submissions are not open yet

When Brightbarrow is ready to read work from beyond the house, this page will say so. Until then, please do not send manuscripts. If you have a practical question about the imprint, a short email is welcome.
