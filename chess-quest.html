CHESS QUESTPALADIN'S PATH

HOW TO PLAY

Arrow keys / WASD — walk

Z / Enter — talk, confirm, attack

X / Shift — cancel

C / Esc — menu (save, items, quests)

On a phone, use the d-pad and A / B.

You are Heinrich “Rich” Witz, a Paladin pawn in an ancient war of order and chaos. Complete the five books of the Chess Quest: Pawn, Knight, Rook, Bishop, and Sovereign.

THE CHESS QUEST

A 16-bit RPG adapted from the novels by Michael D. Young.

Book I — Paladin: Pawn

Book II — Nemesis: Knight

Book III — Rook: Revenge

Book IV — Bishop: Betrayal

Book V — Sovereigns: Song

Rich Witz receives a chess set and learns he is heir to the Paladins, knights of order. With his guide Aaron he faces Nemesis knights, the Gray Queen Temperance, and the Corridor of Keys — where his father vanished.

Fan adaptation for play. Story, characters, and setting belong to their author.
