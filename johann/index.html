Johann

dein Deutschlehrer · your German tutor

Hallo! Ich bin Johann.

I'm a German tutor who works alongside whatever course you're taking: a textbook, a school or university class, an exam course, or an app. I'll explain things, chat with you in German, check your writing, and quiz you. I won't do your homework for you, though. I'll help you get to where you can do it yourself.

1 · Connect Johann

Johann runs on Claude, an AI model by Anthropic. Your teacher may have given you a school link and class code. If not, you can use your own Anthropic API key.

Your key is saved only in this browser and is sent only to Anthropic (api.anthropic.com). Don't use a shared or public computer with your own key. Usage is billed to the key's account.

2 · Your course

Tell Johann what you're studying so he can match your level and your current unit. If your teacher sent a course code, paste it here.

3 · About you (optional)

Your level, your background with German and other languages, your goals and interests. Johann uses this to pitch his German at the right level and pick topics you care about. You can also do a quick level check with him.
